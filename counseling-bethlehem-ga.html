Counseling for Bethlehem

A short drive. A long arc.

Bethlehem is the kind of place where neighbors still know each other by name, where Sunday morning matters, and where a hard season can feel isolating because everyone seems to be doing fine. Our Statham office is 7 miles south — a 12-minute drive — close enough to come weekly without it being a project. For Bethlehem families, we offer what a small town often doesn't: a confidential place to bring what you can't say out loud at church or in your own kitchen.

If the drive doesn't work for you, we also offer Zoom sessions for Bethlehem residents anywhere in Georgia — same counselors, same conversation, just from your own home.

Why people in Bethlehem choose us

Real care. Real privacy. Real affordability.

We've sat with Bethlehem-area families through divorces that nobody saw coming, through the long grief after a parent or child is gone, through marriages where both people had run out of things to try. Our work is Christ-centered — Rev. Jeff is a board-certified Christian counselor with 35+ years in ministry — but we won't preach at you. We sit with you. When cost is hard, our sliding scale and scholarship fund are real.

We don't accept insurance — here's why. Our pricing is transparent: a set fee per session, with a sliding scale (based on family size and income) and a Counseling Scholarship Fund for those who need it. We never want money to be the reason someone goes without help. The fund is donor-supported — you can give to the fund.
