Grief counseling · Barrow County, GA

You don't have to carry this alone.

One-on-one counseling and a free monthly support group for anyone walking through loss in Barrow County and surrounding areas.

Take the first step

Grief isn't a problem to solve. It's a season to walk through — with someone beside you.

Whether you're walking through the loss of a spouse, a parent, a child, a friend, a marriage, a job, or a future you'd imagined — it's heavy. And grief doesn't keep a schedule. It shows up in waves, on anniversaries, on ordinary Tuesday afternoons. You're not behind. You're not broken. And you don't have to do this alone.

Two ways to begin

Whatever fits the season you're in.

One-on-one counseling

Private sessions with Rev. Jeff or Stephanie. Talk at your own pace. Bring whatever you need to — tears, anger, questions, or silence. No script, no timeline. Available in person at our Statham office or by Zoom.

Request a first conversation

Encounters in Grief group

A monthly gathering for anyone carrying loss. Free. Open to all. You can come once. You can come every month. You can sit and not speak.

When: 2nd Sunday of every month, 3:30 PM

Where: 2064 Highway 82, Statham, GA 30666

Cost: Free

Let us know you're coming

Who you'll be walking with

35+ years walking with families through loss.

Reverend Jeff Langley holds a Trauma & Grief Coaching certification through Light University in addition to 35+ years of pastoral counseling and board certification with the International Board of Christian Care.

He's walked alongside hundreds of families through loss — spouses, parents, children, sudden tragedies, slow declines. He knows grief doesn't move in straight lines. And he knows that being present matters more than having the right words.
