# Encounters in Joy — Brand Voice Guide

A practical reference for writing copy, posts, emails, brochures, or any
visitor-facing content for Encounters in Joy, Inc.

---

## 1. Brand personality

If Encounters in Joy were a person, they'd be a **wise older friend who has
walked through hard things and come out the other side.** They speak gently
but directly, never preach, and they always make room at the table. They
know Scripture but quote it like a friend sharing something meaningful, not
like an expert reciting a passage.

---

## 2. Voice attributes

Five attributes define how we communicate. Each has both **what we are**
and **what we are NOT**, with examples.

### Warm

- **We are:** empathetic, compassionate, gentle, never rushed
- **We are not:** sappy, performative, or trying to manufacture emotion
- **Sounds like:** *"All are welcome — whatever you believe, wherever you've been."*
- **Does NOT sound like:** *"We are SO excited to walk this journey with you!!"*

### Honest

- **We are:** transparent about pricing, scope, credentials, and limitations
- **We are not:** evasive, marketing-speak, or hiding behind jargon
- **Sounds like:** *"There is a set fee per session, due at time of service. We don't accept insurance."*
- **Does NOT sound like:** *"Investment in your healing is structured for accessibility."*

### Faith-grounded, never preachy

- **We are:** rooted in Scripture, comfortable naming our convictions, welcoming to those who don't share them
- **We are not:** evangelistic in our marketing, doctrine-heavy, or gatekeeping
- **Sounds like:** *"You don't need to belong to a church — or believe what we believe — to be welcomed and helped here."*
- **Does NOT sound like:** *"Have you accepted Jesus as your personal Lord and Savior? If not, click here to learn how!"*

### Plainspoken

- **We are:** clear, simple, direct; we use the word people would actually say
- **We are not:** clinical, corporate, or padded with filler
- **Sounds like:** *"Reach out today."* / *"Send a message."* / *"Take the first step."*
- **Does NOT sound like:** *"Initiate your therapeutic intake process at your earliest convenience."*

### Calm

- **We are:** measured, never urgent, never alarming
- **We are not:** hype-driven, exclamation-heavy, or fear-based
- **Sounds like:** *"We respond within 24 hours."* / *"A real person responds within 24 hours."*
- **Does NOT sound like:** *"Don't wait! Schedule TODAY before slots fill up!!"*

## 3. Audience

**Primary audience:** Adults in Barrow County, GA and the surrounding 5
counties (Jackson, Gwinnett, Oconee, Walton, Clarke) who have been
**struggling for months** with anxiety, depression, grief, marriage problems,
trauma, or life transitions — **not in acute crisis.** They are often
hesitant, curious, sometimes embarrassed to reach out.

**Secondary audience:** Pastors, friends, and family members researching
counseling options on someone else's behalf.

**What they care about:**
- Will I be judged?
- Can I afford it?
- Is this real care, or just church people giving advice?
- Will my conversation stay private?

**Level of expertise:** Zero. Assume no familiarity with counseling
terminology, therapy modalities, or biblical counseling specifically.

**How they expect to be addressed:** Like a neighbor. Not "Dear valued
client." Not "Beloved." Just plain, warm, second-person ("you") English.

## 4. Messaging pillars

Five core themes we keep returning to. Every piece of copy should reinforce
at least one.

| # | Pillar | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Experience matters** | "35+ years of pastoral counseling, board-certified" |
| 2 | **Welcome without conditions** | "All are welcome — whatever you believe, wherever you've been" |
| 3 | **Cost should never be the barrier** | "Set fee per session, with sliding scale and counseling scholarship fund" |
| 4 | **Real conversation, not a sermon** | "Faith-grounded, never forced" |
| 5 | **You take one step; we walk with you** | "Take the first step" + "We'll walk alongside you" |

Hierarchy: pillar 2 (welcome) and pillar 5 (one step at a time) carry the
most emotional weight. Pillars 1 and 3 carry the rational/trust weight.
Pillar 4 prevents the brand from feeling exclusively religious.

## 5. Tone adaptation by context

Voice stays fixed. Tone dials attributes up or down by situation.

| Context | Tone adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Homepage hero** | Calm + warm dialed UP; honest dialed slightly down (no fee specifics here) |
| **FAQ answers** | Honest dialed UP, plainspoken dialed UP — questions get direct answers |
| **Intake form policies** | Honest + plainspoken dialed UP — this is the legal/practical surface |
| **Grief group description** | Warm dialed WAY up; calm dialed up; faith dialed down (let the grief lead) |
| **Crisis routing** | Calm dialed up; warm dialed up; faith dialed off (not the moment) |
| **Pricing copy** | Honest dialed UP; warm just enough to feel human |
| **Social media (Facebook)** | Warm dialed up; calm stays; plainspoken stays; faith CAN come forward |

## 6. Style rules

### Capitalization
- **Sentence case** for all headings, including H1, H2, H3, FAQ questions
  - ✅ "What does it cost?"
  - ❌ "What Does It Cost?"
- **Title Case** only for proper nouns: city names, county names, "Encounters in Joy" (note lowercase "in"), "Reverend Jeff Langley"

### Punctuation
- **Em-dashes** (—, not - -) for parenthetical asides and dramatic pauses
- **Curly quotes** (" ") not straight quotes (" ")
- **Periods at the end of sentences in body copy.** No periods on H3 card headings.
- **Question marks** at the end of FAQ questions
- **No exclamation marks anywhere.** Calm voice = no exclamation marks. Ever.

### Numbers
- Spell out one through nine; use numerals for 10+
  - "five counselors" / "35+ years" / "two appointments"
  - Exception: phone numbers, addresses, dollar amounts always use numerals

### Em-dash use
- Use em-dashes for asides: *"All are welcome — whatever you believe, wherever you've been."*
- Surround with spaces (em-dash with space on either side)

### Lists
- Use periods on full sentences in lists, no periods on fragment lists
- Use serif markers (•) sparingly; prefer numbered or styled lists in HTML

### Contractions
- **Use contractions.** "We're" not "we are." "Don't" not "do not." Friendlier and matches the warm + plainspoken attributes.

### Links
- Link text should describe the destination, never "click here"
  - ✅ "Download the **Counseling Intake Forms**"
  - ❌ "**Click here** to download the forms"

## 7. Terminology

### Preferred vs. avoid

| Use this | Not this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Counseling | Therapy | "Therapy" implies licensed clinical work, which we don't do |
| Counselor | Therapist | Same reason |
| Set fee per session | Pricing structure / Cost / Investment | Honest + plainspoken |
| Sliding scale | Income-adjusted pricing | Plainspoken |
| Counseling Scholarship Fund | Need-based assistance | Brand-specific term, capitalized |
| Take the first step | Get started / Book now | Brand CTA, matches H2's "journey" metaphor |
| Send a message | Submit / Send / Contact | Plainspoken |
| Call & leave a voicemail | Call us | Honest — sets the right expectation |
| 24 hours | 1 day / 1 business day | Concrete |
| Faith-grounded | Christ-centered / Biblical (in context) | "Faith-grounded" is broader; "Biblical" is for SEO H1 only |
| Reverend Jeff Langley | Pastor Jeff / Jeff | Full title for the first mention; "Rev. Jeff" or just "Jeff" later |
| Stephanie Langley | Mrs. Langley / Stephanie | First-name + last-name on first mention; "Stephanie" later |

### Brand-name capitalization

- **Encounters in Joy** — lowercase "in" (the brand uses this style)
- **Encounters in Grief** — same (the support group)
- **Encounters in Families** — same (the support group)
- **Counseling Scholarship Fund** — title case (formal program name)
- **Light University** — proper noun, always full name on first mention

### Words we avoid

- "Just" — diminishes ("It's just $X" sounds dismissive)
- "Simply" — assumes ease the user may not feel
- "Easy" — varies by person; says nothing
- "Best" / "leading" / "premier" — unsubstantiated superlatives
- "Game-changer," "transform your life," "life-changing" — marketing hype
- "Crazy," "insane," "lame" — ableist
- "Guys" as a collective noun — use "everyone" or "you all"

## 8. Inclusive language

- Use **they/them** for unknown individuals
- Use **gender-neutral** language: "spouse" not "husband/wife" in general copy
- Use **person-first** language: "person with anxiety" not "anxious person"
- Avoid culturally specific idioms that don't translate
- "Counseling for individuals, couples, and families" — covers everyone

## 10. Quick examples

### ✅ Good

> *"We charge a set fee per session and don't bill insurance — but we never want cost to be the reason you don't get help. Ask about our sliding scale and counseling scholarship fund, based on family size and income."*

Why it works: honest pricing → empathy → concrete solution. Warm + honest + plainspoken in one paragraph.

### ❌ Bad

> *"Affordable, accessible counseling solutions to help you achieve breakthrough on your healing journey! Contact us today for a FREE consultation!"*

Why it fails: marketing hype, exclamation marks, fake urgency, vague benefits, unsubstantiated free claim (we have a set fee), "journey" cliché without grounding.

## 11. Channel-specific notes

### Facebook posts
- Open with warmth or a question
- Keep under 80 words
- One emoji max (if any) — never sparkles or hearts in a row
- No hashtag stacks

### Email replies to intake submissions
- Address by first name
- Acknowledge what they wrote ("Sounds like you've been carrying...")
- Offer one concrete next step (a time, a phone call window)
- Sign as "Jeff" or "Stephanie" — not "the Encounters in Joy team"

### Website CTAs (button labels)
- **Primary:** Take the first step
- **Secondary text link:** Call & leave a voicemail · 770-807-1227
- **Form submit:** Send my message

---

## Maintaining this guide

When in doubt, ask:
1. Would Jeff or Stephanie actually say this?
2. Would a hesitant person feel more welcome reading this, or less?
3. Are we being honest about what we offer?

If "yes, more welcome, honest" — ship it.
