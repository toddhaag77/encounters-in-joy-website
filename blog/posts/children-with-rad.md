On this blog I want to discuss the topic of Reactive Attachment Disorder better referred to as RAD. This is a disorder which I believe is becoming more prevalant in our society today. It involves the lack of attachment which children need to have with their caregivers in order to form a healthy understanding of self.  My wife and I adopted two such small children ages two and three who we would later find out suffered from a lack of attatchment. We personally had never heard of attachment or the need for it or the consequences of not froming it in early childhood. It did not take us long to discover that our two "precious" additions had significant mental health issues which were eventually traced back to this very same attachment disorder. We knew something was wrong when we found ourselves faced with the daily smearing of their feces throughout the house, along with the ongoing "meltdowns" screaming, cussing and physical violence that attended them. This served as a catalyst for my furthering my education to try and determine what was taking place in the minds of these two children. We understood they had been abused but could not fanthom the scale of that abuse. From the outside they appeared to be two sweet normal little girls, but that is a deception, one which we later learned was purposely presented. In one of our first visits to a psychologist we were told point blank that they were broken and couldnt be fixed and that we needed to return them to the Department of Family and Childrens services. We couldnt believe what we were hearing. In todays world there is a pill for everything, surely there was one which could help these two. To make a long story short he was partially correct. In fact we have discovered over the last fifteen years that there are things when it comes to mental illness, even with all our technology, which cannot be fixed with a pill, nor with extensive therapy. Currently I am in the process of writing a book about our experiences with these children. I have seen books writen about abusing parents etc but what happens when you have a child with a sever psychological problem who abuses the parents? Just this week between myself and my wife we have spoken to two families who have had to endure children which appear to be suffereing from this disorder. I cannot cure the child, but I can help the parents survive the storms which they present. If you are one or you know of someone who has a child like this please have them contact me. The greatest need my wife and I had throughout these years is to just have somone listen to us who did not think we were crazy. Looking forward to meeting with you! God Bless, Jeff
