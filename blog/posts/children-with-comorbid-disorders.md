In our lasts blog we discussed Reactive Attachment Disorder which affects children who are raised in a disfunctional environment. It normally is observed by the age of five and often is misdiagnosed by therapist. One disorder that is often diagnosed along with it or instead of RAD is bipolar disorder. That has become, at least in this writers view, a go to diagnoses. It has been my personal experience that while the child does in fact exhibit characteristics found in being bipolar there are some who list the same characteristics as part of RAD. The caution I have here concerns the medications which are given to treat bipolar may not necessarily stop the manifestations which are occuring due to the RAD. While I do not profess to be a psychiatrist or psychologist when it comes to the use of medications or diagnostics, my personal experience of dealing with two children diagnosed with RAD and then having bipolar added, I believe qualifies me to speak on the subject. In fact my experience is that children with RAD may in fact end up with multiple diagnoses as did mine. Attachment disorders are gaining more attention these days than when we first encountered this some fifteen years ago. At that time there was limited knowledge and practically no therapist who were trained or understood this disorder. I remember that I once insisted that a local therapy group of which I will not name. provide a trained therapist in RAD to treat my daughter. When no progress appeared to be taking place I inquired as to where this particular therapist recieved training and was told  "I read a med students article on line"' I immediately fired the therapist. I now only know of a couple of certified RAD therapist currently, While I do not consider myself trained in working with the child, I do consider myself qualified to work with the parents of the child who find themselves shunned, isolated, and many times blamed for the actions of the child which can include running away, distroying class rooms at school, lying, stealing, and on and on. I have personlly had to deal with all of the above. If you or someone you know is dealing with a child who has been diagnosed with RAD please have them contact me, I assure you I understand what they are going through. God Bless, Jeff
