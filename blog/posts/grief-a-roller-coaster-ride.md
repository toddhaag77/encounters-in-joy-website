Today is 911 a day which like D-Day with live in infamy. Lives were lost, hopes dashed, and the world changed. Some might say is has been twenty five years surely those who lose love ones would be over it by now! The fact is we never get over it, whether it is a tragedy along the lines of 911 or the death of a loved one we never get over it and here at Encounters In Joy we do not want you to get over it. Our goal is to help you live with it and help you to understand the roller coaster ride that you will experience many times for years to come. Grief is universal but in its universality we all experience it differently, unique to us and our personality, upbringing and life experiences. That means no two roller coaster rides are the same. We are here to help you with your ride! Give us a call or contact us to discuss your experiences and how do learn to make it one day at a time. Next week we will be posting more about this ride to help you understand what you are going through. God Bless, Bro. Jeff
