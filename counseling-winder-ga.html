Counseling for Winder

A short drive. A long arc.

If you live in Winder, our Statham office is right next door — just 8 miles west along Highway 82, about 15 minutes door-to-door. We're in the same county you are, and we know the rhythm of life here: the high school football games on Friday nights, the families putting down roots near Fort Yargo, the small businesses lining downtown. Why people in Winder choose us

Real care. Real privacy. Real affordability.

Many of our clients from Winder choose us specifically because we're not part of a big clinical system. There's no waiting list, no insurance intake process, and no diagnosis filed in your medical record. We work on your schedule, with whatever pace fits your life. And because cost is the most common barrier people quietly carry, we built a sliding scale and a Counseling Scholarship Fund so it doesn't have to be.

We don't accept insurance — here's why. Our pricing is transparent: a set fee per session, with a sliding scale (based on family size and income) and a Counseling Scholarship Fund for those who need it. We never want money to be the reason someone goes without help.
