Why we exist

Our mission

Our mission is to provide quality, faith-grounded counseling to individuals, couples, and families as they navigate the complexities of life — and to make that care available to all, regardless of their financial situation.

Encounters in Joy is a Christian counseling ministry, not a clinical practice. We believe everyone encounters seasons that leave them drained and overwhelmed — and that in those seasons, we all need someone to come alongside us. Not to judge, criticize, or condemn, but to listen and to help.

Our work is rooted in Scripture, but we meet you where you are. You don't need to belong to a church — or believe what we believe — to be welcomed and helped here.

Our name comes from the apostle James: “Count it all joy when you fall.” — James 1:2

What we do

Our programs and services

We carry out our mission through counseling, support groups, and financial help for people who couldn't otherwise afford care.

Individual and couples counseling

One-on-one or couples sessions for personal growth, marital issues, trauma, and grief.

Family and youth counseling

Whole-family sessions, plus faith-based support for children and teens navigating life's challenges with age-appropriate care.

Trauma and grief care

Specialized care for loss, traumatic events, and the long road of healing. Learn about our grief support →

Encounters in Grief support group

A free monthly gathering for anyone carrying loss — the second Sunday of every month at 3:30 PM, at our Statham office.

Encounters in Families support group

A group for families, forming now. Send a message to join.

Sliding scale and Counseling Scholarship Fund

Counseling has a set fee per session. A sliding scale based on family size and income, and a donor-funded scholarship fund, keep cost from being the reason someone goes without help.

We meet with people in person at our Statham office, by Zoom, or in groups — and we serve people across Barrow, Jackson, Gwinnett, Oconee, Walton, and Clarke counties.
