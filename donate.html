Support the ministry

Help keep cost from being the barrier.

Encounters in Joy is a 501(c)(3) counseling ministry. Counseling here has a set fee per session — and because people give, cost doesn't have to be the reason someone goes without help.

Your gift funds the sliding scale and the Counseling Scholarship Fund — so a neighbor walking through grief, anxiety, or a struggling marriage can get real help, whatever their income.
